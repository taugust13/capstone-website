Protect the Salmon

Protecting salmonid species means protecting waterways and the web of connections they support. By protecting salmon, we are also protecting the land and waters that we all depend on to survive. Human developments and activities has led to the loss of many salmon-bearing streams in Vancouver, leaving only a few wild salmon tributaries remaining today.

Negative Human Impacts

  • Urban developments increase stormwater runoff which carries sediment and pollutants into creeks and can smother salmon eggs. Sedimentation in tributaries poses a serious risk of irreversible damage.
  • Culverts and storm sewers create impassible obstacles for spawning salmon to reproduce.
  • Litter and unleashed pets along trails harm creeks and streams.
