Musqueam Cultural Significance

Salmon hold deep cultural significance for the Musqueam people.

  • Salmon are not viewed merely as a resource, but as relatives and as life-givers among the animals.
  • The salmon life cycle can teach us that life is always moving, evolving, and renewing. Each stage of life has a purpose.
  • Through their journey to the ocean and return home, salmon shows us reciprocity by how they give back to the land, waters, and other beings.

The land is our oldest Elder. If the land and the inhabitants are lost, the stories and knowledge connected to those places and animals will be, too.
