The Gift of Salmon

In Musqueam teachings, salmon are not simply seen as a resource, but as beings, relatives, and lifegivers who bring life to the land and its inhabitants. Their journey to the ocean and return home teaches reciprocity, as they sustain the waters, forests, and the many beings that depend on them. The salmon life cycle reminds us that life itself is constantly changing, evolving, and renewing, and that we are all connected.

When habitats are destroyed and the inhabitants are gone, the stories and knowledge connected to them will too vanish with time. Our teachings shape our cultural identity and the way we encounter the world around us.
