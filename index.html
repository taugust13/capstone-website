The Importance of Salmon

When salmon populations decline, it disrupts the natural balance. Many plant and animal species lose important sustenance, while streams, rivers, and forests become less productive and biodiversity declines.

These impacts are felt not only by the environment, but also by coastal Indigenous communities who have lived alongside, cared for, and learned from salmon and countless other beings since time immemorial. For the Musqueam people, whose traditional and unceded territory extends throughout much of what is now known as the Lower Mainland of British Columbia, salmon are more than just a food source. They are teachers, relatives, and an integral part of Coast Salish way of life and culture.
